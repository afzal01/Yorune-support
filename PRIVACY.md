# Privacy Policy for Yorune

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Yorune does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

All habit data you create — habit names, completion status, streaks, and 
progress — is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is sent to any 
server, and Yorune does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking 
SDKs of any kind.

If you delete the app, all associated data is permanently removed from 
your device.
