# Yorune Support

Yorune is a simple, private habit tracker for iOS.
Track daily habits, build streaks, and monitor your
progress over time.

Your habit data stays on your device.

## FAQ

**Does Yorune require an account?**

No. Yorune does not require an account.

**Is my data backed up if I lose my phone?**

No. Habit data is stored locally on your device.
It will not transfer automatically if you change
devices or delete the app.
